**Claim 1:** Backshoring decisions are linked to quality, coordination, and flexibility problems experienced at the (offshore) production site.
- Driver vs. implementation barrier: Primarily a **driver** of the backshoring decision (a problem motivating the return) rather than an observed implementation barrier of the return process itself. - Firm-level vs. macro-level: Firm-level (case-based).
- Asia-specific: Partial — the corpus explicitly says the paper "includes a China-related case," but the available search snippets do not confirm which case is the China case or attribute this specific finding to it. Treat as Asia-relevant only in a general, unconfirmed sense.
- Suggested framework category: Cost/Operational (quality, coordination, flexibility issues at the offshore site) — tentative, pending firm/case-level confirmation.
- Verification status: PARTIAL — the general finding is attributable to a search excerpt, but its link to a specific (China) case is UNVERIFIED.

**Claim 2:** Post-backshoring, firms face a depleted domestic supply base, vanished manufacturing skills, and higher domestic labour and input costs in the home (high-cost) country.
- Driver vs. implementation barrier: **Implementation barrier** — this describes a difficulty encountered in re-establishing production domestically after the decision has been made, which fits the thesis's definition of implementation barriers (difficulties in transferring, rebuilding, integrating, or stabilising production).
- Firm-level vs. macro-level: Presented at a general/aggregate level in the excerpt ("high-cost countries" generally) rather than tied explicitly to one of the four named cases in the retrievable snippet — likely firm-level in the original paper, but the search snippet itself reads as a general statement rather than a specific case citation.
- Asia-specific: No — this describes conditions in the (high-cost) home country, not in an Asian host country. Not Asia-specific by the thesis's definition (which requires linkage between an Asian host and the return home).
- Suggested framework category: Asset/Capability (vanished manufacturing skills, depleted supply base) and Cost/Operational (higher labour/input costs) — this claim plausibly spans two framework categories.
- Verification status: PARTIAL — attributable to a search excerpt that reads as a close paraphrase of the source, but not confirmed as a verbatim quote, and not tied to a specific case/country in the available material.
