## What could be established from available search snippets
Confidence: abstract-level / framework-level only. No full-text findings could be extracted (publisher and DIVA full-text are network-blocked).

The paper is a review article that organizes prior reshoring literature into a **conceptual reshoring framework of five sequential themes: (i) antecedents, (ii) contingencies, (iii) decision, (iv) implementation, and (v) outcome.** This five-theme structure was returned consistently across multiple queries (the title+abstract query and the "research agenda process outcome" query) and is the paper's central contribution. The authors "organize and discuss past literature, propose a research agenda for each single theme and new avenues for future research on the conceptualization of reshoring as a process." This confirms the source treats reshoring as a *process*, with implementation as an explicit but under-developed stage — matching the corpus rationale that "implementation is underexplored."

One indexed snippet (query: "transaction cost resource-based view … implementation underexplored") stated that governance mode — whether reshored manufacturing is conducted **in-house or outsourced to a supplier** — is "one under-researched aspect of reshoring," and that contingencies such as **industry and product type** shape implementation and outcomes. This governance/in-house-vs-outsource point is plausibly attributable to A01 but appeared in a results block that also surfaced a *different* paper ("A multi-theory framework for understanding the reshoring decision," 2021), so I treat it as partial.

IMPORTANT ATTRIBUTION CAUTION: The same query returned a sentence claiming the work "employs the eclectic paradigm, the resource-based view and transaction cost economics as a theoretical basis, highlighting the value of integrating location-specific factors …". That phrasing strongly matches the separate 2021 *International Business Review* paper "A multi-theory framework for understanding the reshoring decision," NOT necessarily Pedroletti & Ciabuschi (2023). I could not confirm from any clean snippet that A01 itself is built on TCE/RBV. Do not cite A01 as a TCE/RBV source without checking the full text.
