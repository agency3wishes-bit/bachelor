## What could be established from available search snippets
Confidence: abstract-level plus a few detailed sentences from indexed abstract/ProQuest excerpts. Full text not accessible.

**Design:** Qualitative **multiple-case study of four companies in the fashion industry** (footwear and clothing). One indexed excerpt specified the sample as "a Portuguese and three Italian firms." The unit of analysis is the firms' offshoring *and* reshoring relocation processes.

**Research aim:** To investigate what types of mistakes occur along the decision-making and the implementation process, and how those mistakes affect the outcome (success vs. failure) of a relocation strategy — i.e., whether "doing the right thing" (correct decision) or "doing things right" (correct implementation) matters more.

**Key finding (consistently returned):** Mistakes occur not only at the decision-making stage but also at the **implementation stage**; "all cases except one reported mistakes at the implementation stage either for offshoring or reshoring," and "the failure of relocation initiatives emerged to be directly connected to mistakes made in the implementation phase." The managerial takeaway stated in the excerpts: "**doing the things right is more important than doing the right thing**." This is directly relevant to the thesis's implementation-barrier focus.

**Asia link:** The corpus rates Asia specificity as *Medium* and its rationale states "some cases involve China." Search snippets confirmed the fashion/footwear-clothing setting and the Portugal/Italy home countries but did **not** independently confirm which specific case(s) offshored to China/Asia or disaggregate findings by host country. A separate e-bike China-to-Italy paper repeatedly surfaced in the same result sets and must not be conflated with A02.
