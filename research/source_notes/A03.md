## What could be established from available search snippets

From search snippets I could establish:
- The paper is a systematic review consolidating and disambiguating terminology across manufacturing relocation research (offshoring, reshoring, backshoring, nearshoring, etc.), based on 158 reviewed papers.
- It proposes a "novel decisional framework" intended to cover "all relevant aspects of a relocation decision," aimed at reducing conceptual fragmentation and inconsistent definitions across the field.
- One search snippet indicates the paper's typology treats reshoring as an umbrella term encompassing both backshoring (relocation directly to the home country) and nearshoring (relocation to a proximate country), regardless of whether the receiving operation is an owned facility or an external supplier.
- The paper's stated motivation is that manufacturing-relocation research still "lacks maturity," with conflicting results across industrial/regional contexts that had not previously been consolidated.

I could **not** establish from available snippets: the specific internal structure/labels of the "decisional framework" categories (e.g., whether it is organized as a "why/how/where/when" typology or some other structure — one search query attempted to confirm this but returned no usable detail); any Asia- or China-specific case content; any explicit discussion of implementation barriers as distinct from decision-stage factors; or any quantified findings. This matches the corpus's own classification of the source as "primarily decision-oriented," i.e., conceptual/terminological rather than empirical.
