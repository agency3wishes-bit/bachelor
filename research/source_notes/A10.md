Search results (OJS Sinergie listing, Semantic Scholar, ResearchGate abstract snippets) consistently describe the paper as an **explorative literature review of 105 articles and book chapters** indexed on Scopus and published up to August 2018, examining the relationship between the back-shoring decision and two sustainability pillars — **environmental** and **social**. The review conceptualizes sustainability under three lenses: (1) motivation/driver, (2) result/outcome, and (3) barrier/enabling factor for back-shoring strategies.
