## Full-text verification (PDF received 2026-07-15; stored at resources/sources/A10.pdf)

**VERIFIED at full text — the sample-size "contradiction" is explained: both figures appear in the paper itself.** The abstract describes "an extensive literature review of 105 articles and book chapters indexed on Elsevier Scopus and published up to August 2018," while the methodology reports **96 documents found → 33 selected** for the analysis (listed in Appendix I). The two earlier conflicting notes were each reading a different part of the same source; the apparent inconsistency is internal to the paper (broader corpus vs. analysed selection). Consequence unchanged: the thesis cites neither figure, and given the paper's own ambiguity, that remains the safest course. (2018a, b) within the review, as the note anticipated ("closure of plants" appears in the barrier content).
