**Barrier-side codes identified in the docx (all coded as Review evidence, not direct empirical, and explicitly NOT Asia-specific):**
1. `DIGITAL_TECHNICAL_SKILLS_SHORTAGE` — shortage of staff able to operate automated systems, manage data, and integrate manufacturing with services. Category: Asset/Capability (+ Human Resources). Stage: Planning → Implementation.
2. `DIGITAL_ORGANISATIONAL_ADAPTATION` — need to change routines, interaction structures, responsibilities, information flows, and customer/supplier relationships, not just buy technology. Category: Cultural/Organisational (+ Asset/Capability). Stage: Implementation.
3. `DIGITAL_ECOSYSTEM_PARTNERSHIP_GAP` — digital servitisation requires interaction with technology providers, data specialists, suppliers, customers, and research institutions; the docx frames this as an "enabling condition" whose absence becomes a barrier rather than a barrier in its own right. Category: Asset/Capability (+ Cost/Operational). Stage: Planning → Reintegration.
4. `OFFSHORE_SUNK_COST_INERTIA` — sunk costs create inertia against the decision to leave the offshore location; the docx explicitly cautions these are NOT the same as new domestic fixed-investment costs and should not be conflated with them. Category: Cost/Operational. Stage: Decision/Exit.
5. `OFFSHORE_KNOWLEDGE_DEPENDENCE` — firm may depend on knowledge accumulated by the foreign supplier/offshore facility/local network/host-country technical specialists; the docx flags that the exact source context needs checking before treating this as a central barrier. Category: Asset/Capability (+ Knowledge). Stage: Exit → Transfer.
