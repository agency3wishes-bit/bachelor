## What could be established from available search snippets

Full text could not be retrieved (publisher domains — Emerald, ResearchGate PDF mirror, Aalto repository PDF — are blocked in this environment; only search-engine snippets/summaries were available). Based on multiple convergent WebSearch results (Emerald abstract page, Aalto Acris repository description, ResearchGate listing):

- The study investigates the extent, drivers, and conditions of backshoring among Finnish manufacturing firms, based on a survey fielded in autumn 2015, and situates Finland's backshoring level against international relocation patterns. One secondary summary cites a sample of 229 Finnish manufacturing firms; this specific figure appeared in AI-generated search summaries only and could not be cross-checked against the primary text or a second independent secondary source, so it is UNVERIFIED and should not be inserted into the thesis without confirmation from the actual article.
- Reported findings (converging across at least two independent search summaries): firms that had backshored production to Finland were more commonly found in industries with relatively higher technology intensity, were typically larger, and operated a higher number of plants than firms with no relocation movement.
- Backshoring firms more commonly reported having a corporate-wide strategy guiding production-location decisions (i.e., backshoring linked to deliberate corporate location strategy rather than ad hoc decisions).
- Firms reportedly brought production back mostly from Western Europe (especially other Nordic countries) and Eastern Europe, with China also mentioned as a source location in one summary — this gives A29 its "Medium" Asia specificity rating in the corpus, since China appears among the origin countries but is not the exclusive or dominant focus of the study.
- Backshoring activity in Finland (a small, open economy) is reported as comparatively higher than found in earlier studies conducted in larger economies.

All of the above points are drawn from secondary search-result summaries (abstract-level descriptions), not from a verified reading of the full article, tables, or exact statistics. They should be treated as PARTIAL SUPPORT at best for any specific quantitative claim, and as CONTEXT ONLY if used to characterize the general drivers/extent of backshoring in a small open Nordic economy.
