## What could be established from available search snippets
Confidence: **abstract-level, plus a few indexed-excerpt details of uncertain reliability** (flagged below). Full text not retrieved.

The study is a **longitudinal qualitative case study** applying a **business-network / internationalization-process lens** (Uppsala tradition — commitment and embeddedness constructs). It examines **partial reshoring** — reshoring only *part* of the activities previously outsourced to foreign suppliers — and its effects on the firm's host-country network. The case involves relocation of activities **from China back to Italy** (consistent with the corpus "China-to-Italy" description).

Headline findings, appearing consistently across the ScienceDirect abstract and IDEAS snippets (high confidence, abstract-level):
- Partial reshoring produces **"concurring but contrasting effects"**: the firm's **tangible commitment and structural embeddedness** in the foreign (host) market **diminish**, while its **intangible commitment and relational embeddedness increase**.
- The result is a host-country network with **"fewer but deeper relationships."**
- Partial reshoring **"may harm the firm's business relationships in the host country and restrain access to essential resources and capabilities."**
- Reducing the activities conducted by foreign suppliers can **lower the level of interaction and make the reshoring firm be perceived as an unreliable partner**, hampering suppliers' trust and mutual orientation.
