## What could be established from available search snippets
Confidence: abstract/press-summary level only. No full-text findings, page-numbered quotations, or verified theoretical framing could be extracted (Springer, ResearchGate PDF, and the SOAS eprints mirror were all inaccessible in this environment).

Multiple converging search results (Springer Link abstract, University of Birmingham research portal, RePEc/EconPapers, ResearchGate abstract) confirm the following at DIRECT-support level for the *stated purpose and case* of the paper:

- The paper is a qualitative single-case study of **Burberry**, the British luxury clothing brand, examining changes in leadership, business model, and supply chain strategy **from 1997 to early 2016**.
- Burberry's business-model change (repositioning toward a brand-led, heritage-focused, "quintessentially British" identity) is described as the trigger for **consolidating and rebuilding manufacturing activities back in the UK**.
- The paper frames this as a case of reshoring driven by **value renewal**: increased managerial control over the supply chain, combined with **close proximity of design and manufacture**, is presented as enabling the brand promise of quality and provenance.
- The renewal of supply-chain strategy is associated (in the paper's own framing, per the abstract-level summaries) with increases in Burberry's revenues and brand value over the period studied.

## Full-text verification (PDF received 2026-07-15; stored at resources/sources/A15.pdf)

**VERIFIED at full text — with one CORRECTION to the docx-derived coding.**
- **CORRECTION — LABOUR_MIGRATION_POLICY_CONSTRAINT does not exist in this paper.** The only "migration" content is the migration *of manufacturing* offshore; there is no labour-migration or immigration-policy constraint on domestic manufacturing anywhere in the text. The docx code was a miscoding. The thesis's Chapter 4 contextual sentence citing A15 for this point has been removed, along with the Table 1, Appendix B, and evidence-matrix entries.
- **Confirmed:** 23 semi-structured interviews (docx figure verified); the Burberry case 1997–2016; the craftsmanship/heritage/provenance driver (interview quote September 2015: "it's the craftsmanship and heritage… buying into"); factory closures incl. New Jersey; the "Keep Burberry British" campaign; Chinese *consumers* (not production origin) valuing Made-in-England — corpus Asia rating Low validated.
- **The exclusion of A15 from the skills/machinery themes (1.1/1.2) is validated:** no machinery-gap or firm-attributed skills-shortage implementation evidence appears; the paper is driver/strategic-renewal evidence, exactly as the thesis uses it.
