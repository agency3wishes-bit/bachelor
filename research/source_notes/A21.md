## What could be established from available search snippets
Search results consistently and specifically describe the paper's method and findings. The authors developed two Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP)-based tools for the initial screening of manufacturing reshoring decisions: one based on traditional (crisp) AHP and one based on fuzzy-AHP. Six high-level, holistic reshoring criteria — described in one search-engine synthesis as "cost, quality, time, flexibility, innovation, and sustainability" — were identified through a literature review and used as the criteria set. A panel of experts from a single Swedish manufacturing company performed pairwise comparisons of these criteria to derive priority weights, which were then applied in a weighted-sum evaluation of 20 reshoring scenarios. The reported result is methodological: the fuzzy-AHP tool's rankings were "slightly closer" to the expert panel's own judgments than the traditional AHP tool's rankings, supporting the suitability of AHP-based tools (especially fuzzy-AHP) for initial screening of manufacturing reshoring decisions.

Confidence level: Moderate-to-high for the overall method description (two AHP variants, six criteria, one Swedish expert panel, 20 scenarios, fuzzy-AHP outperforming traditional AHP) — this level of detail appeared with reasonable consistency, though the specific six-criteria list ("cost, quality, time, flexibility, innovation, sustainability") came from an AI-synthesized search summary rather than a directly quoted abstract sentence, so it should be treated as plausible but not fully confirmed verbatim. Low confidence for any of the 20 scenario contents, industries involved, or whether any scenario involved an Asian host country.
