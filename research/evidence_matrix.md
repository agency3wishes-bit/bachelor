# Evidence Matrix — Reshoring Implementation Barriers × Sources

Consolidated from the 33 source notes in `research/source_notes/` (WebSearch pass + `source_coding_notes.docx` enrichment). This is the drafting basis for Chapter 4 (Results) and the source-to-claim reference for Part C review checks.

## How to read this matrix

- **Evidence types are kept separable** per the corpus workbook's decision rule 2: *Direct empirical* (barrier observed in the source's own primary data), *Review* (barrier reported through a literature review), *Conceptual* (barrier theoretically argued). Never sum them into one count.
- **Verification tags** carry over from the source notes: DIRECT / PARTIAL / UNVERIFIED. Almost all docx-derived detail is PARTIAL (high-confidence secondary coding, not page-verified primary text). No page numbers may be cited for any source — none are verified.
- **Asia-specific = Yes** only where the source explicitly ties the barrier to an Asian host in a return-home case (corpus decision rule 3). Only A13 (China→UK) and A18 (China→Italy) qualify. A04 (2 of 4 cases returned from China — full text verified: SkaItalia and Roncato) is *partial*; **A02 is NOT Asia-attributable — full text (verified 2026-07-15) shows hosts Romania, Morocco, Croatia, Hungary; the docx's 'China main offshore location' claim was an error**; A09 (China/Poland present) is *no* for most barriers, EXCEPT the exit-regulation barrier, where the full text (verified 2026-07-15) explicitly names "strict layoff regulations in their offshored locations, such as Poland and China" — Partial/named for theme 4.1.

## Open contradictions & hard constraints (carry into every chapter draft)

1. **A10 sample size:** 105 articles (WebSearch) vs. 33-from-96 (docx) — unresolved; cite neither figure.
2. **C05 value-chain direction:** docx version (US chains shortened/domestic content rose, with Chinese value-added rerouted via third countries) supersedes the WebSearch "lengthening" claim if C05 is cited in Introduction/Discussion.
3. **C03 country percentages** (China ~40%, USA ~63% etc.): unconfirmed by both passes — never cite. The aggregate 28.5% relocation-magnitude figure is confirmed but is not a firm-count.
4. **No page numbers exist for any source.** A06's NotebookLM pages come from a postprint; A18's (35/49/58/80) are flagged as not real journal pages. No in-text page citation until PDFs are checked.
5. **Tranfield, Denyer & Smart (2003)** is suggested by the docx as a methods citation but is **not in the approved corpus** — do not cite.
6. **TCE/RBV attribution:** for A01, A02, A06, A07, A09, A15, A16, A17, A18 the TCE/RBV mapping is the thesis's own interpretive overlay, not the papers' declared frameworks (A09 uses OLI; A18 uses network/internationalization theory; A16 builds on Antràs). Discussion must attribute theories to authors only where confirmed, and own the interpretive layer explicitly.
7. **A11 "11.64% of TCA"** figure: VERIFIED at full text (2026-07-15) — static barriers = 11.64% of TCA from low-cost countries, author's survey of 60 large Polish firms (dynamic 47.39%, hidden 40.97%). May be cited as decision-stage driver evidence with that exact framing; never as an implementation barrier.
